Welcome to Fabelo Studio: little ink stories with big hearts.

Meet Scribble, a small ink stick figure with a red scarf and a very big heart. He fails, he falls, he gets back up, and every story he lives is a little bit of ours.

On this channel you'll find:
✏️ Emotional animated short films about failure, hope, kindness and growing up
💡 Animated explainers that make history, science and the human mind easy (and fun) to understand

Every frame is drawn in ink on warm paper. Simple drawings, real feelings.

If a story ever made you smile, tear up, or call someone you love, you're in the right place.

🔔 Subscribe and turn on notifications so you never miss a new story with Scribble.

Fabelo Studio: because every mistake is a page in your story.
